## Format choice: Archie recorded Reel

This article is built on a real number from a real account and a genuine opinion about when not to
run Google Ads. That is exactly the case where Archie's face and voice add something a carousel
cannot. A carousel of the metrics would be fine. Archie saying "one of my clients spends fifteen
dollars a day and got twelve leads last month" is better, because the credibility comes from him.

One recording serves Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. A carousel fallback is at the
bottom if Archie would rather not record.

## The Reel: 30 to 40 seconds

### Objective
Get local service business owners who think they cannot afford Google Ads to book an ad account
review, using one real result as proof.

### Primary hook
"One of my clients spends fifteen dollars a day on Google Ads. Last month, that got him twelve leads."

### Three alternate hooks
1. "Everyone says you need a big budget for Google Ads. Here is a real account that proves otherwise."
2. "If your Google Ads say limited by budget, there is a change coming August 17 you need to know about."
3. "Twelve leads. Fifteen dollars a day. Here is how a small Google Ads budget actually works."

### On-screen headline (burned into the first 3 seconds)
`$15/DAY = 12 LEADS`

### Teleprompter script
Read at a normal pace. This runs about 35 seconds. Every number is from the real handyman account.

> One of my clients spends fifteen dollars a day on Google Ads.
>
> Last month that brought in twelve leads, at about thirty-seven dollars each.
>
> So yes, a small budget can work. But here is what most people get wrong.
>
> When a small budget fails, it is usually not the budget. It is the settings.
>
> Most accounts I audit are paying for the Display Network and Search Partners they never needed.
> A lot of them send ad clicks to their homepage instead of the right page. And a surprising number
> have no conversion tracking at all, so they cannot even tell what is working.
>
> Fix those first. In most accounts, there is more to gain from fixing the setup than from spending
> more.
>
> If you want, I will look at your account and tell you what I find. Link is in my bio.

### On-screen text cues
- 0 to 3s: `$15/DAY = 12 LEADS`
- ~10s: `IT'S THE SETTINGS, NOT THE BUDGET`
- ~20s: three quick bullets appear as he says them: `DISPLAY + SEARCH PARTNERS ON` / `TRAFFIC TO HOMEPAGE` / `NO CONVERSION TRACKING`
- End card: `FREE AD ACCOUNT REVIEW` + brightboxdigital.io

### Shot list
1. Archie to camera, medium shot, for the hook and the close. This is most of it.
2. Optional B-roll or screen recording over the middle section: the real campaign screenshot with
   the client name cropped, and the settings screens he mentions.

### Screen recording suggestions
- The campaign row showing $15/day and 12 conversions (crop the client name)
- The Networks settings toggle where Display and Search Partners live
- The conversion tracking screen showing the misconfigured action

### Reel cover
Headline `DOES $15/DAY WORK?` over a still of Archie or the metric.

### Safe zones
Keep text in the middle 80 percent vertically. TikTok and Reels put UI over the bottom 15 percent
and the top 10 percent. The CTA and headline must clear those.

### Accessible transcript
Same as the teleprompter script above. Add captions burned in, most of this audience watches muted.

### Article link placement
- Instagram: link in bio, plus a link sticker in the Story reshare
- TikTok: link in bio
- Facebook: link in the caption
- YouTube Shorts: link in the description
