## Format rationale

The topic is naturally sequential, and Archie chose not to record a Reel. The carousel turns the
article into a short decision path: the three kinds of proof, a one-take route, how to keep the
recording easy to review, and what to do after a rejection. Every point comes from the published
article and Archie's interview. No approval guarantee is implied.

The design follows the previously approved Brightbox carousel system: one consistent navy, purple,
and amber theme; no small logo; no slide numbers; no fake buttons; centered content; native layouts
for each platform rather than automatic cropping.
