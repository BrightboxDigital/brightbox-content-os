## Format: single image post

The article is a comparison. A carousel or a Reel could also work. I chose a single image post
because the branded image already exists in native sizes for every platform, and Archie hasn't been
asked to record for this one. A 30-second Reel built around his "you're welcome to use it, but..."
answer is a good optional follow-up if he wants to record.

TikTok and YouTube aren't connected in GHL, and a static image doesn't suit either one, so they're
skipped for this post.
