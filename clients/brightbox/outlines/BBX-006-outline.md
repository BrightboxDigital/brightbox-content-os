## Originality gate

**What will this article contribute that a reader cannot get from the current top five results?**

1. **A service-area-business-first workflow.** The top results mix storefronts, online-only
   businesses, and service-area businesses. They do not give a clean path for a contractor or home
   service company that works from a home or private location and does not display its address.
2. **A one-take filming plan, not just an evidence list.** Readers need an order that moves from
   location context to tools or equipment to proof of management without stopping to improvise.
3. **A preflight consistency check and recovery path.** The article will connect the profile's name,
   category, service area, website, signage, documents, and what appears in the video, then explain
   how to diagnose a rejected or stalled submission without inventing a guaranteed fix.
4. **Clear privacy boundaries.** The checklist will identify sensitive information that should not
   be exposed unnecessarily while still showing legitimate evidence.

- A Brightbox decision framework for storefront, hybrid, home-based service-area, and mobile

## Interview result — 2026-10-18

Archie's answers clear the firsthand portion of the originality gate. He has helped multiple service
businesses through video verification and has personally had submissions fail despite showing the
requested evidence. His experience supports four additions to the draft:

- Keep the recording short and make each proof point easy to see.
- Show the requested evidence, then add clear legitimate proof such as signage, paperwork, business
  cards, equipment, entry access, or website administration access where relevant.
- Do not promise that a complete video will pass on the first attempt.
- After repeated failures, describe Archie's experience with seeking human review, while separately
  verifying Google's current official support path and avoiding a guaranteed outcome.

There is no publishable screenshot or client case, so none will be invented or implied.
