Founder, Brightbox Digital. Fort Wayne, Indiana.

Who I am

I started Brightbox Digital in 2022 and went full time in the summer of 2023, when I left my engineering job. The whole thing began because a friend asked me to build him a website. That turned into enough positive feedback from clients that I decided the career I had was not the one I wanted.

Brightbox is a one person business, and I keep it that way on purpose. When you hire Brightbox, I am the one doing the work and the one you talk to. That means I see every project end to end, from the first conversation about what a business actually needs through to what the site does six months after launch.

The engineering background shows up more than I expected it to. Most of this work is diagnosis before it is design. Figuring out why a site is not producing leads is closer to debugging than it is to decorating.

What I work on

I serve local small businesses in Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, along with clients around the country. Most of them are service businesses, contractors and home service companies, and professional service providers who need their website to bring in work.

  • Website design and development
  • Search engine optimization and local SEO
  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Google Ads and Facebook advertising
  • Logo design
  • Social media marketing

What I write about here

I write the articles on this site myself. They come out of work I am actually doing, which means they tend to cover the questions clients ask me repeatedly rather than whatever topic is trending. Where I have an opinion, I say so and explain why. Where something is genuinely uncertain, such as how AI search will change local visibility, I say that too instead of guessing confidently.
