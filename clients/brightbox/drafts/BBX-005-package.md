# BBX-005 Delivery Package — AI Website Builder vs. Professional Website Development

Draft v1, 2026-10-16. Status: **Archie Review Needed**. Not published, not in WordPress.

## 1. Article summary

This article helps a local business owner decide between an AI website builder (Wix Harmony,
Squarespace Blueprint AI, GoDaddy Airo) and hiring a professional. It's built on Archie's interview
answers:
- You're welcome to try one.
- It fits tech-savvy owners who need one to three informational pages.
- The setup, SEO, domain and hosting work around the AI is where the time goes.
- Builder platforms limit layouts unless you write code.
- He uses AI himself, including for mock-ups and his own point-and-click builder, and doesn't charge
  much less for it, because the finished product is the same.

Primary sources back it: Google's generative AI content guidance (updated 2026-10-01), and the Wix
and Squarespace help docs on export and lock-in. About 1,950 words of body text. Fair to both sides
by design, since most of the ranking pages are written by someone selling one side.
