## 4. Human readable preview

**AEO, GEO, and llms.txt: What Google's Guide Says**

Two questions keep coming up from clients: do I need an llms.txt file, and is AEO/GEO different from
the SEO we're already doing? Google answered both directly in a July 10, 2026 update to its official
AI optimization guide. On llms.txt: "Doing so will neither harm nor help your site's visibility or
rankings in Google Search, as Google Search ignores them." On AEO/GEO: "optimizing for generative AI
search is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO."

The article quotes both answers exactly, explains what an llms.txt file actually is for readers
unfamiliar with the term, adds an honest qualification that Google's statement is specifically about
Google Search and doesn't speak to other AI systems, and points to Google's new Search Console
Generative AI performance report as the real way to check AI visibility, including its real current
limits (impressions only, still a limited rollout). It closes with what Brightbox actually tells
clients: the same SEO fundamentals as always, on-page, off-page, and technical, since the underlying
approach hasn't changed even as some specific tactics are still being worked out industry-wide.

Archie's own material runs through the piece: his actual honest answer when clients ask about
llms.txt, his consistent "it's still just SEO" response on AEO/GEO, and what he tells clients to
focus on instead of chasing either topic. No invented case study or client example, since Archie
confirmed he doesn't have one yet.
