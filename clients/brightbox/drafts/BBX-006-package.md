## 1. Article summary

This article gives service-area businesses a practical Google Business Profile video verification
plan. It explains the three kinds of evidence in Google's current instructions, turns them into a
one-take recording route, separates a hidden public address from the address used for verification,
and gives a documented retry and support path.

Archie's experience is used where it adds something the ranking pages do not: he has helped several
service businesses, has had his own videos fail despite showing the requested proof, tells owners to
keep the recording short and clear, and seeks human support after repeated failures. The draft does
not claim that support or any checklist guarantees approval.

## 4. Human-readable preview

The article answers the problem in this order:

1. Google controls the available verification methods.
2. The profile and real-world proof should agree before recording.
3. A service-area video must establish location, business operations, and management.
4. A planned one-take route is easier to review than a long improvised tour.
5. A home address can remain hidden from customers while still being used for verification.
6. Rejected videos should follow Review issues, retry the missing proof, and use support after the
   available methods keep failing.

## 10. Social and GBP distribution preview

These are direction previews only. Full posts are built after the article is live and validated.
Nothing has been sent to GHL.

**GBP direction:** Video verification is not about making the longest video. It is about showing the
right proof in one clear recording: where the business operates, what it uses to do the work, and
why you are authorized to manage it. The article turns Google's requirements into a service-business
checklist.

**LinkedIn direction:** I have had Google Business Profile videos fail even after I believed I showed
everything. That is why I do not promise a magic checklist. I plan a short route, make each proof
point easy to see, and keep working through the stated issues. If the available methods keep failing,
I ask support to review the case.

**Facebook direction:** Getting stuck on Google Business Profile video verification? Before you
record again, line up three kinds of proof: location, real business equipment, and proof that you
manage the business. Keep the video short, clear, and continuous.
