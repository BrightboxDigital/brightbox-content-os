## Note on the introductory statistic

Editorial standard calls for a verified statistic or quantitative fact in the introduction where
defensible. None is used here. Every quantitative claim found in competitor content (percentage
weightings for ranking factors) traces back to no accessible methodology and fails
`source-validation.md`'s automatic rejection criteria. Rather than borrow one, the introduction
uses Google's own quote framework instead, and says directly that no such published weighting
exists. This is a deliberate omission, not an oversight.
