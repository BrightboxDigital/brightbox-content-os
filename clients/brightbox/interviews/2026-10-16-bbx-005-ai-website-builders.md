## Answers

Received 2026-10-16, Archie's words lightly cleaned for filler only. Quote from this, never beyond it.

**Q1. What owners say / what he tells them.**
He always tells them they're welcome to use it. But there's a lot more to web design than talking
to the AI. You need to know how to set it up properly, at least the basic SEO (which you can learn),
hosting, domains, "figuring out all that." "With the amount of time you spend getting stuff right,
you might as well pay somebody." "You're always going to find somebody that thinks they can do
something cheaper, easier, quicker." He's all for people trying it themselves: "If they can succeed,
more on them." He'll still get clients, because plenty of people don't know how to do this stuff,
"especially older people." Analogy: why doesn't everybody start their own business? Because not
everybody is made to do it or up for it.

**Q2. Who should just use an AI builder.**
You need to be at least somewhat tech savvy, and you want a quick informational website: one page,
maybe two or three. "Any further than that, I don't think you should really use an AI builder,"
because it's something you need to learn yourself.

**Q3. Taken-over builder sites.**
Yes, he has taken over Wix, Squarespace and GoDaddy sites. Not an AI-builder site yet. On all those
platforms you're very limited in customization: limited in which sections you can have and the
specific designs, and you don't really have the option to fully customize the look the way you want.

**Q4. Test build.**
Yes. He would do it, framed as "hey, this is what your website could look like." He already uses AI
builders to generate mock-ups of new websites for prospects. He also built his own AI builder with a
point-and-click editing tool, so that if he ever hands a site off to a client they can make changes
themselves.

**Q5. Cost.**
No price range given. When he uses an AI builder he doesn't charge much less than normal, because
he's still delivering the same final product: an SEO-optimized website that talks about the
business and does what they need. "Just because I'm able to get there in a quicker way now doesn't
mean that the value of my work is lesser."
