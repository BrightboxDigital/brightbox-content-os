## 1. Experience helping service businesses verify

Archie has helped multiple service businesses through video verification. In his experience, it can
help to show the front of the business, any signage, someone unlocking the door, access to the
website's back end, and business equipment.

He has also personally failed video verification multiple times even after showing everything. It
is not a fixed or guaranteed process. Sometimes a business has to keep working through it because a
human does not review the submission until it is escalated.

## 2. Evidence Archie recommends

If the business has signage, paperwork, business cards, equipment, or similar proof, Archie
recommends showing it. The video should cover the requested evidence and include extra proof where
it can be shown clearly.

## 3. The most common mistake

One of the most common mistakes is failing to keep the video short and failing to show everything
quickly and clearly. The business needs to cover what Google requests without making the evidence
hard to follow.

## 4. What to do after a failed or stalled verification

Archie recommends continuing through the verification process. If repeated attempts do not work,
he recommends escalating the case so a human can review it. In his experience, the verification
normally works after that human review.

This is personal experience, not a guarantee. The article must describe it that way and must check
Google's current official support path before giving procedural instructions.
