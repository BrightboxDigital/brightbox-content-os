## Rules

- Three to five internal links per article.
- Natural descriptive anchor text. Never "click here" and never an exact match keyword stuffed anchor.
- Link to the most specific relevant page. Prefer a service page over the homepage.
- Do not link the same URL twice in one article.
- Do not force a link where it does not serve the reader.
- Same tab.
- After publishing, add at least two links from existing relevant pages into the new article and
  record them in the tracker.
