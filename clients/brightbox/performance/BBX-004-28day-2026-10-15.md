## Diagnosis

**This is mainly an indexing and health check. It's too early, and the numbers are too small, to say
anything about performance.** Two impressions before and two after can't tell you whether the refresh
helped. The position jump from 6.5 to 62.5 is based on two impressions each and is just noise. It
isn't a ranking drop.

The 90-day baseline showed 29 GA4 sessions, much more than the 28-day windows on either side. Those
sessions came before mid-August and had already stopped before the refresh. The refresh didn't cause
the decline. I didn't track down where those earlier sessions came from.

The page is healthy: indexed, canonical correct, meta description fixed, and linked from the service
page. Nothing in this data calls for adding words or keywords. Don't do that.
