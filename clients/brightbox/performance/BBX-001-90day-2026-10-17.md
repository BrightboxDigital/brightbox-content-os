## Diagnosis

The page is indexed, technically healthy, internally linked, and already appearing around the first
page on a very small number of impressions. The constraint is demand and visibility volume, not a
technical failure. Twelve impressions are not enough to diagnose a title or description problem,
and zero clicks at that volume is not enough to justify a rewrite.

GA4 shows visits and engagement, but no key events. That does not prove the article cannot convert;
the sample is too small. It does mean there is no conversion evidence to use as a success claim.

**No content change is recommended from this check.** Adding words or keywords would not address the
observed constraint. Leave the article intact and compare it with a later, like-for-like 90-day
window before deciding whether the framing needs to change.
