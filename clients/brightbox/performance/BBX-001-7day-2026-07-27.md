**These are not directly comparable.** The baselines are 90-day pre-launch aggregates for the
whole site or the whole legacy blog section; the article figures are an 8-day window for one new
page. They are shown side by side for context only, not as evidence of progress. The article has
no pre-launch baseline of its own — it did not exist before 2026-07-19 — so this 7-day pull is
itself now the first recorded data point for the page (baselines.csv row added 2026-07-27).

**1 GSC impression at 7 days is normal, not a problem.** New pages typically take longer than a
week to accumulate meaningful impression volume. The single impression at position 10 is a
positive early signal — it means Google has already indexed and is serving the page for at least
one query — but it is far too little data to judge whether the article is "working." **7 days is
too early to judge performance.** This check exists to confirm the page is technically sound and
indexing, not to evaluate rankings or traffic.
